### Project Background
- A leading technology company specializing in e-commerce solutions, is embarking on a transformative journey to upgrade its e-commerce platform.

***Goal***: Create a cutting-edge e-commerce platform that exceeds customer expectations.

**User-Centric Design**  
Personalized and intuitive shopping experience.   
Design driven by customer behavior and usability principles.  

**Technology Advancements**  
AI and machine learning for better recommendations.  
Data analytics for optimized inventory.  

**Enhanced User Engagement**   
Chatbots for real-time support.  
Social shopping features.  

**Scalability and Performance**  
High-performance architecture.  
Support rapid growth and transaction volumes.

**Risk Monitoring and Mitigation**  
- Highest priority risks added to project tasks.
- Risk managers are assigned to each risk.
    - Responsible for monitoring and actions.
- Bi-weekly status updates to be implemented.
- Creation of automation dashboards for AI models (accuracy, drift) and security.
- Continuous review of risk register as new risks appear in the project.

**Mitigation**  
- Privacy By Design principles to be added.
- AI explainability and bias detection models.
- Increased frequency of integration testing and earlier in the timeline.
- Change Control processes to be added.
- Additional data quality management techniques.
